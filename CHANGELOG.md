# Changelog

What’s new in Graph Paper, newest first. In the app: Settings → About → What’s new.

Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org): a patch (1.0.1) only fixes things; a minor version (1.1.0) adds or changes something you’ll notice; a major version (2.0.0) means something you rely on works differently (the backup file, or syncing with devices on an older version) or was taken away.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-15

The first numbered version. Everything so far:

### Lists
- Nested bullets you can reorder, indent and outdent, with a bar of buttons over the phone’s keyboard and shortcuts on a computer.
- To-dos with checkboxes, or plain notes; numbered lists; folding.
- Paste a markdown list and it keeps its shape. Copy a line, or a line and everything inside it, out as markdown.
- Web addresses in a line are links.

### Links
- Link any bullet to any other with @, across lists. A link shows the item’s name as it is now.
- A line that’s only a link stands for that item: check it off in either place.
- An item’s page shows what links to it and what each link means (“buy at”, “waiting on”), and facts you state yourself.
- Places, people and your own kinds of things, each with its own page and tab.
- A graph of any item and what’s around it, with numbered lists side by side as lanes.

### Getting around
- Search everything with Ctrl-K, open a line’s menu with Shift-Ctrl-K, and move or send lines without the mouse. ? lists the shortcuts.
- To-dos inside: every to-do in a line or a page, however deep, in one list.
- Vim keys, if you want them.

### Your lists
- Works offline, installed from the browser. Your lists live on your device.
- Export and import a backup file, and a trash you can bring things back from.
- Sync between your own devices through a server you run yourself.
- Looks for updates when you come back to it, or when you ask.
